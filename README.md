# Library-of-Daniel
The Library of Daniel exists to gather, preserve, and integrate fundamental knowledge of humanity so that ANY humans may prosper with less suffering, less errors, and true wisdom. The digital library exists to cultivate a person who: Thinks clearly.  Acts ethically.  Seeks truth without fanaticism.  To provide resources for education and knowledge.
